# Original spell sound effects

These finite sound effects were synthesized specifically for Draconia by `tools/build_spell_audio.py`. They contain no third-party recordings or samples. The WAV assets are dedicated to the public domain under [CC0 1.0](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/); the synthesis source follows the project's MIT license.

The manifest records each deterministic seed, waveform hash, duration and measured peak. Every source clip peaks at −9 dBFS. Native playback uses spatial attenuation, finite voice limits, event deduplication, pause/mute controls and a separate −3 dBFS safety limiter. The effects remain a development sound pass, subject to listening and mixing review.
